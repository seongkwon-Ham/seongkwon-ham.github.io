Thank you for visiting. I am an M.A. student in Economics at Yonsei University, working on the macroeconomics of climate policy.

My research asks where the cost of carbon goes once a policy is in place: how a carbon price passes through the power sector to production and jobs, how a trade agreement changes the carbon embodied in trade, and how financial markets reward carbon efficiency. Alongside my studies I work as a research assistant at the Korea Economic Research Institute and as a teaching assistant at Yonsei University.
